- 2026-11-20 surprise Root cause of the `simple_iter` gap (diagnosis by systematic debugging): not the per-run fetch. Binding a fresh fetch per archetype changed nothing (50.4 us either way). The cause is `TableStorage::set_ref`/`set_mut` building the slice with `map_or(&[][..], ..)`: a required column's fetch length became "len or 0", so the optimizer lost that the three slices are `len` long. Assembly (`cargo rustc --release --emit asm`, codegen-units=1): the hybrid step had 13 pairwise alias checks, two of them between the position array and itself at offset 4 and 8, which always hold, so the vector loop was never entered and the run was scalar; M3.4's step has one alias check and the same vector loop is taken. With `expect` in place of `map_or` the time fell to 24.4 us.
